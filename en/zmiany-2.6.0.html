Changes in version 2.6.0

Download Mod

Version 2.6.0 from 20.11.2026. A new layout for the character window and the mercenary panel (with the mercenary's Attack Rating), coloured resistance names, separate gold and light radius blocks in “Advanced Stats”, and Warlock skills that grow with their level. Character saves and charms in the pouch stay. Updating from 2.5.0: extract the archive into the mods folder in the game folder and replace the files. Changes in the previous version: 2.5.0.

Character window

  • A new background and group layout: Damage, Attack Rating, Defense, Life and Mana in separate frames with even spacing.
  • No Stamina – Dexterity sits next to Attack Rating, Vitality next to Life.
  • With spells (no attack rating value) the window shows “Attack Rating” and “-” instead of an empty field.
  • Resistance names in element colours: fire red, cold blue, lightning yellow, poison green.

Mercenary panel

  • A new look in the style of the character window: a frame with the name, level and experience bar, the items below it.
  • Table: Strength and Damage, Dexterity and Attack Rating, Life and Defense, then resistances.
  • A new Attack Rating field for the mercenary (from dexterity and item bonuses).
  • Bigger skill icons, aligned with the name frame.

“Advanced Stats” window

  • Extra gold and light radius in separate blocks.
  • Scrolled to the bottom, the list ends level with the window frame.

Warlock

  • Flame Wave: width 9 / 11 / 13 and range 30 / 35 / 40 yards at levels 1 / 10 / 20.
  • Ring of Fire: 8 / 16 / 32 flames and range 7 / 9 / 11 yards at levels 1 / 10 / 20; the description shows the number of flames and the range.
  • Apocalypse: radius 12–17 yards, bigger every 4 levels (1 / 5 / 9 / 13 / 17 / 20).
