Blacksmith Tools

Back to features

Tools for the Horadric Cube: five from blacksmiths, two from Terror Zones. All of them take 2 cells and work on any base.

From blacksmiths (Hell only)

ToolBlacksmithPriceIn the Cube
PliersFara, Act II50 000empties sockets, their contents are lost
TongsHalbu, Act IV100 000with a magic or rare item and a perfect gem they make a crafted item
HammerCharsi, Act I500 000turns a white item into a rare one
DrillLarzuk, Act V750 000adds sockets: white 1–maximum, magic, rare and crafted 1–2, uniques and sets 1
Flat ChiselHratli, Act III1 000 000upgrades the base tier: normal → exceptional → elite

Prices after completing the blacksmith quest on Hell; before that Charsi, Fara and Hratli charge about 10% more, and Larzuk twice as much. On Hell the tools are always in stock.

From Terror Zones

ToolWhere fromChanceWhat it does

The File is the rarest item in the game. The number of players does not change the Axe chance.

Crafts as in Project Diablo 2. Any base of the given type fits (quivers too). Every recipe gives 3 fixed stats (some 4) and 1–4 random ones; an ethereal item gives an ethereal result. The exact stats are shown in the perfect gem’s tooltip in the game.
