Changes in version 2.7.0

Download Mod

Version 2.7.0 from 20.11.2026. A new, consistent colour scheme in the character window, the mercenary panel and “Advanced Stats”: names in element colours, white numbers, negative values in dark red. Character saves and charms in the pouch stay. Updating from 2.6.0: extract the archive into the mods folder in the game folder and replace the files. Changes in the previous version: 2.6.0.

Character window and mercenary panel

  • Negative resistances in dark red (easier to tell the value from the name).
  • Life and Mana labels in colour: life red, mana blue (also the mercenary's Life).
  • Remaining stat and skill points in white instead of red.

“Advanced Stats” window

  • Names in element colours, values in white: absorb, mastery, monster resistance, lightning thorns.
  • Resistances: dark red when negative, gold at the maximum, otherwise white.
  • Extra damage: the whole line in the element colour.
  • Leech and On kill: the word life red, mana blue, numbers white; life regeneration red, mana recovery blue.
  • Freeze blue, poison length reduction green, “no” grey, immune red.
  • Magic find: words in quality colours, numbers white.
  • Item penalties: numbers in dark red.
  • Speed breakpoints and the active player count in orange; plain numbers (gold, light radius, experience, kills) white.
