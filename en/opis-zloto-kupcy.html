Gold, vendors and mercenaries.

Gold limitIn the mod
Inventory999 999 (at every character level)
Personal stash30 000 000
Shared stash2 500 000 per page (as in the game)
  • At vendors (buying, gambling, repair) and when hiring a mercenary you pay only with gold from the inventory – gold in the stash does not count.
  • After death all gold in the inventory is lost; gold in the stash stays.
  • With “Auto pick up gold” the character picks up gold from 1.5 times farther away.
