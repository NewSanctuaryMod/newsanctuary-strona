Charms only work in the Charm Pouch.

Charms

  • A magic charm can have any 2 different stats from the whole list, e.g. fire resistance and lightning resistance.
  • Small, large and grand charms can also drop as rare (yellow): at most 2 prefixes (defensive and offensive) and 2 suffixes (stat and damage type).
  • The strongest tiers (e.g. +20–30 defense, all resistances, +13–17 mana, +16–20 life, +6–7% magic find) and +1 to a skill tree on a grand charm are magic only.
  • A magic large charm (1×2) with item level 70 or higher can roll +1 to one random skill of one class, e.g. “+1 to Fist of the Heavens (Paladin)” (required level 60). It is a rare stat, at most one per charm, never on rare charms.
  • The Hellfire Torch always gives +3 to its class’s skills and does not cast Firestorm on striking.

Charm Pouch

Where to buyAkara, Act I – about 100 000 gold, always in stock
Size6 × 6
How many you can haveone
What fits in itcharms only
When charms workonly in the pouch, while the pouch itself is in the inventory
  • The name is turquoise, like magic items: Charm Pouch.
  • It opens with a button on the bottom bar (when it is in the inventory) or with a click in the stash. A charm dropped onto the pouch goes inside.
  • The pouch cannot be put in the shared stash – it returns to the personal one.
  • You can identify charms in the pouch with a scroll or tome from the inventory.
  • At a vendor you can open the pouch and the Cube to move items (Ctrl + LMB then moves instead of selling).
