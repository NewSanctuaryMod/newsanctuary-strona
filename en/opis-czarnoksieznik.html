Changes to Warlock skills. Everywhere the skill level counts together with bonuses from items.

Bind Demon

Bind Demon levelCan bind
1normal demons
5+ champions
10+ unique minions
15+ uniques
20+ super uniques

Any demon can only be bound once it has at most half of its life. The skill tooltip shows a “You can bind: …” line.

  • A unique that had an aura (e.g. Conviction) keeps it. A demon without an aura gets a random one. Hephasto never gets Conviction.
  • The demon’s aura is remembered – after re-entering the game it stays the same.
  • The Bind Demon level is checked every time you enter the game. A level that is too low removes bonuses and lowers the demon’s type (unique → minion → champion → normal); a super unique below 20 disappears. Once you are back at a high enough level, the demon regains its bonuses and aura.
  • The demon’s life after re-entering is recalculated for the current number of players in the game.
  • Hovering over the demon’s portrait shows its name, type, traits, skills, aura, life, defense, damage, resistances and skill bonuses.
