Every tab has a 4 × 4 Cube in its lower part. The stash panel is as tall as the inventory, and gold sits in the same place in both stashes.

  • “Move to stash” (left arrow, before the gold): with the stash open it moves all gems, runes, skulls, materials and Runeword Tomes from the inventory onto their stacks. Gold is not touched.
  • “Sort items to the right” (right arrow, under the grid): one click pushes items to the right side – in columns, larger ones first, identical items next to each other. Items already sorted stay in place.
  • Gold has its own button next to the amount: with the stash open it opens the deposit, without the stash – dropping gold. You can type the amount right away.
  • All gold amounts and prices are spaced every three digits, e.g. “1 000 000”, including gold lying on the ground (“927 388 gold” – the number in the same color as the text). The “Max gold” hint above the gold in the inventory and stash uses the gold counter color.
  • Vendor and blacksmith prices end with “gold”: cost, sell value, repair cost and repairing all items (e.g. “Cost: 3 502 gold”).

Bottom bar

  • 14 buttons instead of 7, in two rows: Character, Inventory, Skills, Quests, Automap, Party, Menu, plus new ones – Mercenary, Horadric Cube, Charm Pouch, Show filtered items, Show all items, Walk/Run and Chat.
  • A button glows gold while its window is open and is gray when closed. When you enter the game all of them are off – you turn on what you want.
  • The buttons work with any key binding (they read Options → Controls). Chat opened with the button does not close after you send a message.

  • A shorter experience bar, split into 10 parts. The tooltip also shows level progress in percent.
  • The mana orb with the demon sits at the right edge of the screen, just like the angel with the life orb on the left.
  • Skill selection: the left and right mouse buttons open one shared panel. Every skill (auras and summons too) can go on both the left and the right mouse button.

Effect icons

  • The middle of the bar shows icons of active effects (up to 9 at once) with a countdown. Passing time darkens the icon like a clock hand.
  • Hovering over an icon shows the effect’s name, what it gives and, for skills, the level and values (e.g. “Enemy defense: -82%”).
  • 67 effects have icons: Sorceress armors, curses, warcries, Druid shapeshifts, Assassin buffs, Warlock effects, shrines, plus freeze, poison, slow, stun, open wounds, burning and more. Auras and healing potions have no icons.

Automap

  • The automap has 3 steps (automap key or bar button): first press – map in the corner, second – large map in the middle of the screen, third – map off. Esc closes the map at once. The corner map stays on the side you choose in the game options (Gameplay → Automap size).
  • The game remembers bar buttons separately for every character: the automap (the large one too), “Show filtered items” and “Show all items” stay on after loading (portal, waypoint, act change), after death and after re-entering the game, if they were on before. A new character starts with them off. Bar buttons work the same regardless of the keys bound in Controls. The “Show Items” key works in every mode from the game options: with “Toggle” it turns the funnel on and off, and with “Hold” and “Timed” it shows items only for a moment. While a panel button is lit, everything works as with “Toggle” regardless of the option – hovering over a monster shows its life, and the key turns the buttons off.
  • The automap only shows chests that need a key (in the mod they are always locked); coffins and other unlocked containers have no marker. Super chests and chests with quest items have a sparkle marker, the town stash a coin, bookshelves their own book marker.
  • The text in the top right corner starts right at the screen edge: mod name, difficulty, “Location:” and “Zone level:”. Terror Zone info only appears once you enter the zone.

Vendors and mercenaries

  • Vendor windows have no gold box – the amount in your inventory counts. The bottom of the blacksmith and other vendor windows is redesigned (one chest with a grate, repair buttons on the sides).
  • The hiring window fits 10 mercenaries. Select a mercenary with a click and hire with the “Hire” button and a confirmation.
  • The mercenary also wears gloves, a belt and boots. The mercenary panel is rebuilt, without empty slots.
  • You can give a potion to the mercenary from the belt and from the inventory: Shift + right click, or left and right click at once.
  • Loot filter: you type the gold threshold (1–100 000) instead of moving a slider; potions are arranged in pairs and in their own colors.

Advanced stats

  • The “Advanced Stats” window (in the character window, and next to the mercenary panel – for the mercenary) is bigger and has fixed lines like in Project Diablo 2: resistances, absorb, damage reduction, masteries, life and mana steal, magic find (separately for magic, rare, set and unique items), extra gold and light radius from items, movement speed in yards per second and more.
  • Lines are grouped into blocks with a header, one value per line: speeds and breakpoints, run/walk, damage reduction, thorns damage (also damage returned in % from skills), weapon mastery, on-hit effects, resistances (also physical, capped at 50%), absorb, masteries, enemy resistance, added damage, leech, on kill, regeneration, freeze, magic find and the rest.
  • Breakpoints for faster cast rate, faster hit recovery, block and attack speed (IAS) are listed in full – the reached breakpoint in orange. IAS breakpoints are calculated for the weapon in hand – its name, type and speed are shown above them.
  • At the bottom: players p1–p8 breakdown, kill counters, play time and number of deaths – in this game and in total for the character.
