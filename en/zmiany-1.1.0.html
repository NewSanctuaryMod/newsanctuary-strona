Changes in version 1.1.0

Download Mod

Version 1.1.0 from 13.11.2026 – all changes from that day. Install it like the previous versions (contents of the zip into the game folder, replacing files). Character saves stay.

Camera

  • Smooth zoom: every mouse wheel step zooms in or out by the same amount, and the camera glides smoothly to the new view.
  • The game remembers the zoom separately for every character, also after leaving the game. A new character starts fully zoomed in.

Automap and bottom bar

  • The automap has 3 steps: corner → large map in the middle → off. Esc closes the map at once.
  • The state of the bar buttons and the automap (the large one too) is remembered separately for every character – after leaving and re-entering everything is as it was.
  • The bottom bar buttons work regardless of the player’s key bindings.
  • A new character starts walking instead of running.

Gold

  • Gold on the ground is spaced and has no “pcs” – e.g. “927 388 gold”, in the gold counter color.
  • The word “gold” next to vendor and blacksmith prices: cost, repair cost, sell value, repairing all items.
  • “Max gold” (the hint above the gold in the inventory and stash) in the color of the counter digits.

Name colors

  • Runes, Uber keys, skulls and Uber shards have light gray names.
  • Gray socketed items, ethereal items and runeword bases lying on the ground are gray (before they were sometimes purple).

Worldstone Shards and Terror Zones

  • A new shard tooltip in the form of a list: zone level (+2 / +4 / +6 relative to the character, at most 102 / 104 / 106), the number of random modifiers from a pool of 9, monster density and heralds.
  • Super and Uber shards: there are 2 / 3 times more heralds (together with monster density) and they have more random modifiers.

Runeword Tomes

  • Tomes bought from the shop always turn into the proper tome – also with a full inventory and when other tomes are already in the stash.
  • Tomes no longer have the “Item level” line.

Runes and Materials tabs

  • Fix: some slots showed “Unidentified” (5 runeword tomes, essences and amber shards), even though these items are never identified. Such a slot fixes itself when entering the game.
  • Items from these tabs always go into the stash identified, so the bug will not come back.

Item descriptions

  • Keys: “3 different keys: random portal to one of 3 Ubers (Uber Andariel, Uber Duriel, Uber Izual)”.
  • Figurines: a white line “Put in the Horadric Cube on Hell in Harrogath”, under the line in gray “with the other two figurines opens the portal to the Uber Ancients”.
  • Soulstones: in gray under the line – corrupting an item, summoning the clone with the Hellforge Hammer on Hell in the River of Flame, and the portal to Uber Tristram.
  • Essences: “with the 4 other essences in the Horadric Cube creates the Token of Absolution”.
  • Standards: one white line with the magic find bonus, under the line the caps for Normal, Super and Uber Terror Zones.
  • “Token of Absolution” everywhere, as in the original game.
