The old recipes for sockets, clearing sockets with a Hel rune, upgrading tiers with runes, crafting with a jewel and rune, and the Stone of Jordan recipe are disabled – blacksmith tools replaced them.

  • Ctrl + Shift + click on a stack moves 2 pieces to the Cube at once (keys, perfect gems and perfect skulls 3 each) – as many as the recipe needs.
  • An item with something in its sockets cannot be changed in the Cube – “Transmute” does nothing. Exception: the Pliers, which empty the sockets.

Runewords

  • Runewords are made only in the Cube: an item with empty sockets + runes in any order + a Horadric Runeword Tome with that word → “Transmute”. The runes go in in the right order by themselves, and the tome disappears.
  • Runes put into sockets by hand give only their own stats. Runewords made earlier stay.
  • In multiplayer the runeword is recorded in the Chronicle of every player in the game.

Corruption

  • Any Soulstone + an item (unique, set, rare, crafted, magic or a white base, also ethereal, plus the Torch and Gheed’s Fortune) in the Cube corrupts the item. The stone is used up. Annihilus no longer corrupts.
Corruption resultChanceExample
Extra stat75%Corrupted: +26% to fire resistance
Negative stat25%Corrupted: −15 to strength

Uniques and sets roll from the magic stat pool, everything else from the rare stat pool.

  • A corrupted item can no longer be changed in the Cube (corrupting again, sockets, tier, crafting). You can put runes and gems into existing sockets and make a runeword.
  • An item with something in its sockets and the small unique charm (Annihilus) cannot be corrupted.
