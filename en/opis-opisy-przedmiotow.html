What an item tooltip shows and which colors item names have.

Values and ranges

  • Every stat shows its value and, in brackets, the range it was rolled from, e.g. “+21 to Attack Rating (21-40)”. The value is blue, the range gray.
  • Gems and runes in sockets are added to the item’s stats. Stats the item does not have itself are added as separate lines above “Socketed (N)”.
  • Stats based on character level show the value for the current level and the range from level 1 to 99.
  • Damage and defense lines changed by stats are fully blue, with the range at the end.

Names

  • Magic items show only the base name, e.g. Battle Staff, without prefixes and suffixes.
  • On the ground you can see the number of sockets, e.g. Centurion (1) – in the name color.
  • The second line of a crafted item’s name has the color of the recipe gem (sapphire – blue, ruby – red, etc.), on the ground too.

Hidden lines

  • The mouse hints at the bottom of the tooltip (“Ctrl + LMB…”, “Shift + LMB…”, “Right click to use”), possible base rolls and staff modifiers are gone. The shortcuts still work.
  • Quest items (Wirt’s Leg, Malleus, the Horadric Staff and its parts, the Hellforge Hammer, Flail and Khalim’s Will) show only their name, without stats.
  • Item skill charges (“Teleport: Level 30 (14/30)”) show the real number and refill after repair.
