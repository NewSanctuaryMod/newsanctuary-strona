Changes to shrines.

  • All shrines are single use – healing, mana and refilling ones too. Only wells refill.
  • A second shrine of the same type adds its time to the time left.
  • Buff shrines work at the same time (e.g. experience, combat and skill at once). Curses do not remove them.
  • Active shrines have icons with a countdown in the middle of the bottom bar.
