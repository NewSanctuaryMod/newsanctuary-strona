Worldstone Shards, Terror Zone strength and standards. Everything applies to Hell difficulty.

The highest monster level is 106 (a level 100 character after an Uber Shard). Zones give no experience bonus.

  • A shard used in an act that already has a zone does not open a new one, it changes the current zone’s strength to its own.
  • Density, modifiers and the MF bonus are listed in the top right corner, under the zone name.

Standards

StandardWhere fromMF bonus in zonesRequired level
Standard of Heroes from Uber Tristram (green)Uber Tristram+50%75
Standard of Heroes from the Uber Ancients (red)Uber Ancients+100%85

Works in all Terror Zones in the given game until it is closed. Standards stack (every player can add theirs), but only up to the zone cap. MF from items is separate for every character.

  • Both Standards of Heroes have a new look (with horns and a skull, green and red) and take 2 cells.

Other

  • The new zones message is a list: “Terrorized:” with the zone names and their acts below.
  • The zone entry sound is shorter – only the first hit is left.
