Horadric Runeword Tomes are needed to make runewords in the Cube.

  • New tomes with a gold name: Horadric Runeword Tome. Each covers one category and lists its runewords, e.g. “Wrath: bow/crossbow (4) + Pul + Lum + Ber + Mal”.

A bought or found tome is unidentified – the category is shown after identifying it.

  • All 12 tomes are in the Chronicle (Accessories → Runeword Tomes), with the same list of words as in the inventory.
  • The Merchant’s Armor runeword (Edge in the game; Ith + Tal + Amn) can only be made in a shield with 3 sockets – it is in the “Armor: Shields” tome.
