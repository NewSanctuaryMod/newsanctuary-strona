Small changes that do not fit the other categories.

  • The start screen shows the mod’s artwork with a burning “Diablo II Resurrected – New Sanctuary” title over a hellish city and rivers of lava, and symmetrically in its corners: D2RLoader with its version (top left), the mod name and version (top right), the game name with expansion and version number (bottom right) and the Blizzard logo (bottom left). The loading screen, main menu and pause menu have no animated game logo; of the game’s waiting boxes only “Connecting to Battle.net” on the start screen is left (dimmed screen and the box, without the animated logo) – the others (e.g. while saving) do not cover the screen. The pause menu buttons are in this order: Exit (saves and exits), Return, Options, Filter, Chronicle.
  • Shift + click on a stat’s “+” immediately adds all unspent stat points to it – without the “Are you sure…” window.
  • No key press needed on the start screen: instead of “Press Any Key” it says “Please wait, the game is loading...”, and after 5 seconds (time to look at the mod logo) the game moves on to the character list by itself; a key press skips the wait.
  • The difficulty window appears when you enter the game with an existing character: available difficulties are active and the ones not yet unlocked are greyed out. A newly created character goes straight into Normal, completing Normal adds Nightmare, and completing Nightmare adds Hell.
  • Changes from D2R 3.4 (ladder season 16) work permanently, offline too: Eternity can also be made in bows and crossbows (300–390% damage, +30% attack speed, damage growing with level, indestructible, +5 to skills), a stronger Isenhart set and about 20 buffed uniques (including Griswold’s Edge, Coldkill, Atlantean, Swordguard, Veil of Steel, Executioner’s Justice, Ethereal Edge, Grim’s Burning Dead, Victor’s Silk, Earthshaker).
  • Smooth camera zoom: every mouse wheel step (while holding the “Zoom” key) zooms in or out by the same amount (instead of 5 uneven jumps in the normal game), and the camera glides smoothly to the new view. The “Zoom” key is smooth too. A new character starts fully zoomed in, and a hint below the character says “Hold the zoom key and set the camera with the mouse wheel” until you first change the camera. Each time you scroll, “Camera: N %” briefly appears – 0 % is the closest view, 100 % the farthest, in 5 % steps. The game remembers the zoom separately for every character, also after leaving the game, and never changes it by itself. The view is shifted slightly down, so your character stands in the middle of the visible area above the bottom bar instead of higher up.

  • Warriv no longer says “Good day” as a greeting.
  • D2RLoader fully in Polish. The D2RLoader console is turned off.
  • Mod check: 3 and 11 minutes after starting the game the mod checks that all features work. If something does not work (e.g. after a game update), a window with a list appears.
  • Automatic save backup every time the game starts (20 newest) in “Saved Games\Diablo II Resurrected\mods\InvGrid5_kopie”.
  • Runes, Materials and Tools tab repair: a slot that showed “Unidentified” (e.g. runeword tomes, essences, amber shards) fixes itself when entering the game. Items from these tabs always go into the stash identified.
  • Languages: new items, name colors, tomes and plugin texts are in Polish and English (in other game languages – English).
