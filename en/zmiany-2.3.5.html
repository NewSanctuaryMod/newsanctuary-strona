Changes in version 2.3.5

Download Mod

Version 2.3.5 from 19.11.2026. A wider skill tree, main menu tidy-ups and the “Show Items” key working in every mode. Character saves and charms in the pouch stay. Updating from 2.3.0: extract the archive into the mods folder in the game folder and replace the files. Changes in the previous version: 2.3.0.

Skill tree

  • The skill tree panel is wider – no dark lattice on the sides, a stone frame with a golden line on both sides and wider tabs (all classes).
  • Skill slots and level tiles are spread out with even spacing.

Main menu

  • The stone field below the Play, Create and Delete buttons has a single shade, and the buttons stand with equal small gaps. The character list is a bit longer.

Bar buttons

  • The “Show Items” key works in every mode from the game options: with “Toggle” it turns the funnel on and off, and with “Hold” and “Timed” it shows items only for a moment.
  • While a panel button is lit, everything works as with “Toggle” – hovering over a monster shows its life, and the key turns the buttons off. Your setting in the game options stays.
