Version 2.1.0 from 18.11.2026. A full grid of slots in the skill trees, a new layout for some Warlock skills and shorter button labels. Character saves and charms in the pouch stay. Updating from 2.0.0: extract the archive into the game folder and replace the files. Changes in the previous version: 2.0.0.

  • The skill trees of all classes have a full grid of slots – the free spots are empty, decorative recesses.
