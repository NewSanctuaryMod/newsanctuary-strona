Changes in version 1.3.0

Download Mod

Version 1.3.0 from 15.11.2026. Install it like the previous versions (contents of the zip into the game folder, replacing files). Character saves stay. Changes in the previous version: 1.2.0.

Camera

  • Smaller maximum zoom-out: 50% more terrain than in the game (before: twice as much). At the farthest zoom no black patches of terrain appear in the screen corners any more.
CameraValue
Views20 (19 wheel steps)
One stepabout 6.1%
Closest (new character start)2.5×
Farthest0.82× – 50% more terrain

Skills

  • A new hint under the skill selection panel: “All skills available on LMB and RMB”. The text sits a bit higher and no longer covers the bottom bar.

Standards of Heroes

  • New caps for the magic find bonus in Terror Zones: 100% in a normal zone, 250% after a Super Shard and 500% after an Uber Shard (before: 250 / 500 / 1000%). Both standards share one cap.
  • Tooltip: “Use in your inventory to get +50% (+100%) chance of finding magic items in Terror Zones”.

Advanced stats

  • A new “Light radius” line (from items) under extra gold.
  • Attack frame (IAS) breakpoints in the tooltip on one line, without wrapping.

Item descriptions

  • “Combine … in the Horadric Cube to create …” everywhere, like on gems: shards, essences (“Combine with the 4 other essences…”) and figurines (“Combine with the 2 other statuettes to open the portal to the Uber Ancients”).
  • The Axe has its gray footer again: “Result: Experience grows again up to level 100”.
  • All description changes are also in the English version.
