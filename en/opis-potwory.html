Monster resistances and immunities, and Sunder Charms.

Immunities

  • No limit of two immunities: champion and unique modifiers always raise resistance, so a monster can have three or more immunities.
  • Everything that lowers resistance (curses, auras, skills and items with “−X% to enemy resistance”) works on immune monsters at 1/3 strength. In the game: skills 1/5, items not at all.
  • Example: a monster with 120% resistance, Conviction −85% and items −15% → 120 − 85/3 − 15/3 ≈ 87%.
  • Monster resistance is at most 200% (at least −100%). Curses and pierce are subtracted from 200%.
  • Under the monster’s life bar you can see the resistance next to each immunity, e.g. “Immune to fire: 110%” – already reduced by Sunder Charms.

Sunder Charms

  • An immune monster’s resistance drops to at most 99% (in the game 95%).
  • All six types (cold, fire, lightning, poison, physical, magic) drop from heralds with an equal 1/6 chance (in the game magic only 3%).
