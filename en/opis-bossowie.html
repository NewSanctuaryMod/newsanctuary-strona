Uber Ancients

  • The portal to the Uber Ancients (Summit of the Colossi, in town) needs only 3 figurines: Talic’s Anguish, Korlic’s Pain and Madawc’s Wrath. The Bul-Kathos and Worusk figurines no longer drop.

Each of the three figurines 1/3. More in Terror Zones.

  • Leaving the summit through a portal restarts the fight, death does not. In online games the Ancients do not return to their places when another player joins.

Soulstones and clones

  • The Soulstones of Mephisto (blue), Diablo (red) and Baal (amber): all three in the Cube in Harrogath open the portal to Uber Tristram.
  • A Soulstone + the Hellforge Hammer in the Cube gives a “Summoning”. The clone appears in the River of Flame (Hell) when the first item drops there. Selling Stones of Jordan no longer summons it.
  • The Hellforge Hammer drops from Hephasto on every kill (only one at a time). It cannot be put in the shared stash.

Mini Ubers

  • Uber Andariel (Lilith in the game), Uber Duriel and Uber Izual drop 1 random Rainbow Facet (each type 1/6) instead of organs. They do not drop essences.
  • Uber Duriel no longer stands by the portal – he waits in one of 4 distant spots on the map.
