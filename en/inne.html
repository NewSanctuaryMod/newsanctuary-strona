D2RNS – Diablo 2 Resurrected New Sanctuary

About the project

New Sanctuary is an independent project expanding Diablo II: Resurrected. Its goal is to give players new gameplay possibilities, innovative mechanics and a fresh take on the cult classic Diablo II.

Main changes

  • Bigger inventory, stashes and Horadric Cube.
  • Clearer item descriptions with stat ranges.
  • Blacksmith tools, Horadric Runeword Tomes and the Charm Pouch.
  • Expanded Terror Zones, boss clones and new endgame items.
