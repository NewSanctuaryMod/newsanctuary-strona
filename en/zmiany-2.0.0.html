Changes in version 2.0.0

Download Mod

Version 2.0.0 from 17.11.2026. A big update: skill trees without prerequisites, a new Eldritch tree, potions, always new maps and a new main menu. Character saves and charms in the pouch stay. Updating from 1.6.0: extract the archive into the game folder (replace files) and delete the file d2rl-krowy-bez-wytrzymalosci.dll from mods\New Sanctuary\d2rloader\plugins. Changes in the previous version: 1.6.0.

Character and skills

  • Skills of all classes need only the character level – no need to learn the previous skills in the tree first (trees without arrows). Synergies work as in the game.
  • Warlock Eldritch tree: Blade Warp from level 1, Echoing Strike from 12, Levitation Mastery from 18.
  • Blade Warp range grows with skill level: half of the game’s range at level 1, full at 10, then up to 28 yards.
  • Leap: range at levels 1–10 as in the game, then grows up to 28 yards; landing knocks monsters back in a larger radius.
  • Corpse Explosion knocks monsters back within the blast radius.
  • Shift + clicking “+” next to a stat spends all free points without the confirmation window.
  • Level 100 gives the game’s normal reward (1 skill point and 5 stat points), no extra bonus.

Potions

  • Potion of Luck instead of Stamina Potion: for 2 minutes +50% gold from monsters and +25% magic find, with a timer icon on the bottom bar. It drops like the old Stamina Potion, and the Cow King gives 8 of them (once per difficulty).
  • New Light Rejuvenation Potion (33% life and mana); 2 of them make a Rejuvenation Potion in the Cube. Each difficulty adds one more: on Normal only the Light one drops, on Nightmare Light and regular half and half, on Hell also the Full Rejuvenation Potion.
  • Monster poison and cold last longer (poison about 2.5×, cold 1.5×, at most 10 seconds). Antidote and Thawing Potion last 2 minutes and shorten poison and freeze.
  • Minor healing and mana potions no longer drop and are not sold – the weakest are Light ones. Every potion vendor sells 2 kinds of healing and mana potions: Light and regular on Normal, regular and Greater on Nightmare, Greater and Super on Hell. Healing and mana potions cost 2× more, Antidote and Thawing Potion 500 gold each.
  • New potion graphics – every size has its own vial; Potion of Luck in a yellow vial, Antidote in a green flask, Thawing Potion (and Malah’s Potion) in a blue one, Potion of Life in a red vial.
  • In the charm pouch, below the grey line: restore and effect time of potions and a short description of keys and scrolls.

World

  • Every character load rolls a new map layout on every difficulty, and the old automap is cleared. The loader option “Generate new map layouts” has no effect in the mod.

Menu and windows

  • New main menu: Multiplayer, D2RLoader, Notifications, Options, Cinematics, Credits and Exit in the left column; Play, Create New and Delete below the character list in one stone field, no character conversion icon.
  • Title screen with the mod’s artwork and no “Press any key” – the game moves on by itself (“Please wait, the game is loading...”). No waiting pop-ups (e.g. connecting to Battle.net).
  • The difficulty window always appears, also for a new character; unavailable difficulties are greyed out and the last chosen one no longer glows.
  • Matching buttons in the pause menu, difficulty window and confirmation windows; shorter questions: “Do you want to exit”, “Hold to delete this character”.
  • Character window: a frame above the life orb, like with the stash.
  • “Only one” charms can’t be put into the pouch twice (red field), but you can keep several of the same in the inventory and Cube.

Camera

  • The character stays centred above the bottom bar at every zoom level.
  • New hint: “Hold the zoom key and set the camera with the mouse wheel” – the wheel zooms only while the key is held.

Other

  • The mod check accepts the Steam (3.4.95041) and Battle.net (3.4.95032) game – no game version window.
  • Faster plugin loading at game start.
