What drops and how often. Shard and boss chances are in separate categories.

Number of players

  • The drop chance grows with every real player in the game (in the game – every second one). This applies to monsters, bosses and chests.

Example: a normal monster that drops something in 37.5% of rolls with 1 player.

  • The player count (P1–P8) is disabled in the mod: the /players command does nothing, and the Offline Difficulty Scaling slider in the D2RLoader settings has no effect on the game. A higher P level comes only from playing together with real players over TCP/IP.

Quality and types

  • Low quality items (“Damaged”, “Cracked”) do not drop.
  • Every magic item can have two prefix stats or two suffix stats, e.g. fire resistance and cold resistance – never the same stat twice.
  • Gloves and boots can have 1 socket (e.g. set items – with the Drill in the Cube). A rune, gem or skull gives them the same as in armor – the gem tooltip shows “Armor, gloves, boots” and “Shield, grimoire”.
  • Endgame items – keys, essences, figurines, Soulstones and Shards – only drop after completing all of Hell (Baal killed on Hell).

Runes

  • The game has 30 runes – without El, Eld and Tir. Nef drops instead of them, and upgrading starts with 2 Nef → Eth. In runewords they were replaced: El → Nef, Eld → Eth, Tir → Ith.

10 runes per difficulty, rolled before items. On Hell the Countess also drops the Key of Terror (about 8.6%).

  • The Countess, the Summoner and Nihlathak on Hell do not drop gold – items drop in its place.

Gems

  • There are no chipped gems and no chipped skull – flawed gems drop instead.

Racks and shelves

  • Weapon and armor racks give white items: 33% superior, 67% normal.
  • Bookshelves only give scrolls and tomes of identify and town portal, grimoires and (5%) a Horadric Runeword Tome.
