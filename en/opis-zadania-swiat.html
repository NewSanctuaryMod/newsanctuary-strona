Quests, towns, chests and maps.

Quests

  • You can only go to the next act after completing all quests of the earlier acts (gray in the quest log) – by any route: Warriv, Meshif, the portal after Mephisto, Tyrael, waypoints. Going back is always possible.
  • The portal to the Worldstone Chamber only works when all quests of Acts I–V are gray – so Nightmare and Hell unlock after completing all quests.
  • The Rite of Passage quest (Act V) has no character level requirement (in the game 20 / 40 / 60).
  • In the Stony Field the stones must be touched without a mistake in the order from the Scroll of Inifuss – a wrong stone resets all of them.
