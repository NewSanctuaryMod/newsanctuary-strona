On Hell, instead of a Full Rejuvenation Potion one of the three full potions is rolled (1/3 each). Full potions have round bottles in their own color. Below the gray line of the tooltip every potion shows how long it takes to restore life or mana, or how long it lasts. The key and scrolls also get a short description there. Every potion vendor (Akara, Lysander, Drognan, Ormus, Jamella, Malah) sells 2 kinds of healing and mana potions: Light and regular on Normal, regular and Greater on Nightmare, Greater and Super on Hell. Minor healing and mana potions no longer drop and are not sold.

  • Monster poison and cold are felt more: poison lasts 2.5 times longer (at most 10 s; damage per second as in the game, so more in total), chill lasts 1.5 times longer (at most 10 s). Poison and chill longer than 10 s are as in the game. Before fighting poisonous or icy monsters it pays to drink an Antidote or a Thawing Potion.
