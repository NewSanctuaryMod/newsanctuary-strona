Character and Skills

Back to features

Changes to characters, movement and skills of all classes.

Skill tree

  • Skills of all classes require only the character level – you don’t have to learn the previous skills of the tree first. Synergies work as in the game.
  • The skill tree has no arrows, and its stone background has no vertical streaks.
  • The skill trees of all classes have a full grid of slots – the free spots are empty, decorative recesses.
  • Every row of the skill tree has a tile with the required character level (1, 6, 12, 18, 24, 30). Tiles of levels your character has not reached yet are dimmed.
  • Sorceress, Lightning tree: Thunder Storm is in row 3 and requires level 12, Nova is in row 4 and requires level 18, and Teleport is in row 3 and requires level 12.
  • Warlock, Eldritch tree: Levitation Mastery and Cleave are in row 1 and require level 1, Blade Warp is in row 2 and requires level 6, and Psychic Ward is in row 3 and requires level 12.
  • Barbarian: Leap Attack stuns the enemy it hits for 0.6 seconds.
  • Warlock: Mirrored Blades sits in the left column of the last row of the Eldritch tree. In the Demon tree, Blood Oath is in the first row and requires level 1, and Demonic Mastery is in the second row and requires level 6.

Movement

  • A new character starts walking (all bar buttons off). Running is turned on with the Walk/Run toggle on the bottom bar, and the game remembers the setting for every character.
  • There is no stamina – running does not use it. Stamina stats are removed, and the Stamina Potion became the Potion of Luck.
  • New “Walk/Run” skill (every class): it only moves the character to the chosen spot, in town too, and never attacks. Clicking a monster works like “Force move”.
  • Walk, Attack and Throw have their own icons in the style of the game’s icons.
  • Running no longer removes all defense: a running character keeps half of their defense against melee attacks (in the game – zero).

The level counts together with bonuses from items. Teleport still goes through walls. Enigma no longer increases magic find.

  • Teleport can be used in town. A Druid in werewolf or werebear form uses it without leaving the shapeshift.

In town

  • All auras (Paladin, mercenaries, demons, Druid spirits, items and monsters) also work in town. The mercenary turns on their aura right away, also after being resurrected.
  • Barbarian warcries, Leap and the Warlock’s piercing blade skill work in town.
  • Warlock Eldritch tree: Blade Warp from level 1, the passive Levitation Mastery from 6, Echoing Strike from 12.
  • Blade Warp range grows with skill level: half the game’s range at level 1, full at 10, then up to 28 yards.
  • Leap: range at levels 1–10 as in the game, then grows evenly with each level up to 28 yards; the landing knocks back monsters around the landing spot.
  • Corpse Explosion knocks back monsters within the explosion radius.

Curses

  • Curses stack: several curses can be active on a monster and on a character at the same time.
