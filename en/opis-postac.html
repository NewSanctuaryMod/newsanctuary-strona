Character and Skills

Back to features

Changes to characters, movement and skills of all classes.

Movement

  • A new character starts walking (all bar buttons off). Running is turned on with the Walk/Run toggle on the bottom bar, and the game remembers the setting for every character.
  • There is no stamina – running does not use it. Stamina potions and stamina stats are removed.
  • New “Walk/Run” skill (every class): it only moves the character to the chosen spot, in town too, and never attacks. Clicking a monster works like “Force move”.
  • Walk, Attack and Throw have their own icons in the style of the game’s icons.
  • Running no longer removes all defense: a running character keeps half of their defense against melee attacks (in the game – zero).

The level counts together with bonuses from items. Teleport still goes through walls. Enigma no longer increases magic find.

  • Teleport can be used in town. A Druid in werewolf or werebear form uses it without leaving the shapeshift.

In town

  • All auras (Paladin, mercenaries, demons, Druid spirits, items and monsters) also work in town. The mercenary turns on their aura right away, also after being resurrected.
  • Barbarian warcries, Leap and the Warlock’s piercing blade skill work in town.

Curses

  • Curses stack: several curses can be active on a monster and on a character at the same time.
