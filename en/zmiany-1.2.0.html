Changes in version 1.2.0

Download Mod

Version 1.2.0 from 14.11.2026. Install it like the previous versions (contents of the zip into the game folder, replacing files). Character saves stay. Changes in the previous version: 1.1.0.

Charms

  • A magic large charm (1×2) with item level 70 or higher can roll +1 to one random skill of one class, e.g. “+1 to Fist of the Heavens (Paladin)”.
  • It is a rare stat: at most one per charm, never on rare (yellow) charms, required level 60.
  • Grand charms (1×3) keep the original +1 to a skill tree.

Crafted items

  • The second line of the name (the base) has the color of the recipe gem on the ground too – e.g. Caster purple, Blood red, Hit Power blue.
  • “Extra crafted stats: N” instead of “Extra stats: N”.

Standards

  • New names: Standard of Heroes from Uber Tristram (green) and Standard of Heroes from the Uber Ancients (red).
  • Tooltip: “Use in inventory: +50% (+100%) magic find in Terror Zones”, with the zone caps under the line.

Item descriptions

  • “Use in the Horadric Cube…” on everything that is transmuted (tools, keys, figurines, Soulstones, runes, tomes), “Use in inventory…” on things used with right click (Token of Absolution, Axe, shards, standards).
  • Worldstone Shards: “Use in inventory to terrorize all of Act III:” – with the act number; “2 identical in the Horadric Cube give …” in one line.
  • Perfect gems and skulls: the reroll recipe at the top (3 perfect gems – magic item, 3 perfect skulls – rare item), the crafting recipe in 3 lines below it; no empty lines in the upper part of the tooltip (runes and normal gems too).
  • Runes: “Combine 2 identical in the Horadric Cube to create …” in one line and a shorter runeword description.
  • Soulstones: a white “Use in the Horadric Cube” line; Tongs, Drill and Hammer – one-line description; Axe without the gray line; File without “(cannot be undone)”; figurines and essences in the English version match the Polish one.

Naming

  • “Terror Zones” everywhere, as in the game (also in the game selection texts and in the mod description); “Key of Terror” used consistently.
  • All description changes are also in the English version.
